# Platform Features and Functional Overview

## 1. Platform Architecture and Access
- Multi-company retail management platform.
- Multiple outlets managed under individual companies.
- Role-based access for Super Admin, Company Admin, Management and Staff.
- Company-level data isolation.
- Centralized administrative workflows.

## 2. Point of Sale and Billing
- Product-based billing with quantity and selling-rate entry.
- Automatic bill total calculation.
- Multiple payment methods.
- Invoice generation and receipt printing.
- Hold and resume billing.
- Customer selection during checkout.
- Cross-outlet invoice lookup within the same company.

## 3. Customer Operations
- Customer profile management.
- Customer search and selection.
- Purchase history.
- Customer-linked transaction records.

## 4. Inventory and Outlet Operations
- Outlet-level stock visibility.
- Inventory workflows.
- Stock movement and inter-outlet transfer functionality.
- Company-level outlet management.

## 5. Subscription and Payment Administration
- Subscription plan and subscription management.
- Payment history and outstanding balance tracking.
- UPI, bank transfer, cash and configured online payment workflows.
- Super Admin payment review and verification.
- Platform-level payment gateway settings.

## 6. Attendance
- Staff attendance workflow.
- Location-based verification.
- Selfie capture and verification.
- Administrative attendance visibility.

## 7. Reports and Analytics
- Sales and revenue reporting.
- Payment-method-wise reporting.
- Outlet-level reports.
- Consolidated company reporting.
- Customer and transaction-related reporting.

## 8. Security and Administration
- Authentication and role-based authorization.
- Company-level tenant isolation.
- Protected administrative actions.
- Audit records for relevant activities.

## 9. Development and Validation

The platform was developed and validated in a local development environment. External payment activation, physical printer/tablet testing and other device-specific checks should not be considered verified unless separately tested.

## Confidentiality Statement

This document is a high-level functional overview. It intentionally excludes source code, database exports, private API details, credentials, customer information, internal business data and proprietary implementation logic.

# Retail Operations Management Platform

> Documentation-only portfolio showcase. No proprietary source code or confidential client data is included.

## Project Overview

A multi-tenant retail operations management platform designed to help retail businesses, franchise networks and outlet chains manage their day-to-day operations through a centralized system.

The platform brings together point-of-sale billing, outlet management, inventory operations, customer management, subscription payments, attendance tracking, reports and administrative controls.

## My Role

**Full Stack Engineering Intern**

Contributed to frontend and backend development, database integration, feature implementation, debugging, API integration and local testing.

## Main Features

### 1. Point of Sale (POS) and Billing
- Create and manage customer bills.
- Add multiple products, quantities and selling prices.
- Calculate bill totals and applicable tax.
- Support multiple payment methods.
- Generate invoices and printable receipts.
- Hold bills and resume them later.
- Look up invoices across outlets belonging to the same company.

### 2. Multi-Company and Outlet Management
- Support multiple companies on a shared platform.
- Manage multiple outlets under a company.
- Maintain company-level data separation.
- Support centralized administration and outlet-level operations.

### 3. Inventory and Stock Operations
- View outlet-level stock information.
- Support inventory management workflows.
- Manage stock movement and inter-outlet transfer workflows.

### 4. Customer Management
- Maintain customer records.
- Search and select customers during billing.
- View customer purchase history.
- Associate transactions with customer profiles.

### 5. Subscription Management
- Manage subscription plans and company subscriptions.
- Track subscription payments and outstanding balances.
- View payment history and subscription status.
- Support administrative subscription management and renewals.

### 6. Subscription Payment Management
- Provide payment workflows for company subscriptions.
- Support UPI, bank transfer, cash and online payment options where configured.
- Allow administrators to review and verify submitted payments.
- Maintain payment records and transaction status.

### 7. Payment Gateway Configuration
- Provide platform-level gateway configuration for Super Admin.
- Manage gateway settings and test/sandbox configuration.
- Keep sensitive gateway credentials restricted to backend configuration.

### 8. Attendance Management
- Support staff attendance workflows.
- Capture location and selfie verification during attendance.
- Provide administrative attendance visibility.
- Apply location-based attendance validation.

### 9. Reports and Analytics
- View sales and revenue summaries.
- Review payment-method-wise reports.
- Compare outlet-level performance.
- Access customer and transaction-related reports.
- Support consolidated and outlet-specific reporting.

### 10. Role-Based Access Control
- Super Admin
- Company Admin
- Management
- Staff

Access to application features is controlled according to assigned roles and permissions.

### 11. Security and Auditability
- Authentication and role-based authorization.
- Company-level tenant data isolation.
- Protected administrative operations.
- Audit records for relevant activities.
